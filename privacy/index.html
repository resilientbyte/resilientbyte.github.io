Privacy

This privacy policy explains which personal data is processed when you visit markusbirner.com or contact me, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Danish Data Protection Act (databeskyttelsesloven).

In short: this website uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no embedded content from other companies. The only personal data processed is technical data that the web server needs to deliver the site, and what you send me yourself by email.

Data controller

Markus Birner
Fredericia, Denmark
Email: hello@markusbirner.com

This is a private, non-commercial blog. I have not appointed a data protection officer, as none is required.

Visiting the website (server log files)

The website is hosted by Simply.com, a hosting provider based in Denmark. When you open a page, your browser automatically sends technical information that the web server records in log files:

  • IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested and the HTTP status code
  • the referring page (if your browser sends it)
  • browser and operating system (user agent)

Purpose: delivering the website, keeping it secure and stable, and investigating errors or misuse.
Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR – my legitimate interest in operating a secure website.
Retention: the log files are deleted automatically after 30 days. I do not use them to build visitor profiles or statistics and do not combine them with other data.

Contacting me by email

If you write to me, I process your email address, your name (if you provide it) and the content of your message in order to reply.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR – my legitimate interest in answering your enquiry.
Retention: I keep the conversation as long as it is needed for our exchange and delete it at the latest 12 months after it has ended, unless you ask me to delete it earlier.

Please do not send sensitive personal data (for example about health) by email.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set any cookies and uses no tracking, analytics or advertising technology.

If you use the light/dark mode switch, your choice (“light” or “dark”) is saved in your browser’s local storage so the website remembers it on your next visit. This information never leaves your device, contains no personal data and is only stored because you actively chose the setting. Under the Danish cookie order (cookiebekendtgørelsen), storage that is technically necessary to provide a function you have explicitly requested does not require consent. You can delete it at any time via your browser settings.

Fonts and external content

All fonts, images and scripts are served from this website’s own server. Your browser does not connect to Google Fonts, content delivery networks or other third parties when you visit the site.

Links to LinkedIn and other websites

The website contains ordinary links, for example to my LinkedIn profile. No data is transferred to LinkedIn or any other website before you click such a link. Once you follow it, the privacy policy of that website applies.

Recipients and processors

Simply.com processes the server log files on my behalf as a data processor under a data processing agreement. Emails are handled by my email provider, Proton Mail, also as a data processor. I do not sell personal data or pass it on to anyone else, unless I am legally required to do so.

Transfers outside the EU/EEA

The website is hosted on servers in Denmark. Emails are stored on servers in the EU/EEA. I do not transfer personal data to countries outside the EU/EEA.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you (Article 15)
  • have incorrect data corrected (Article 16)
  • have your data deleted (Article 17)
  • restrict the processing (Article 18)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Article 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Article 21)

To exercise your rights, simply send an email to hello@markusbirner.com. I will reply within one month.

No automated decision-making or profiling takes place on this website.
