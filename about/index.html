About me

Hi, I’m Markus.

I’m an SAP Technical Architect at DLG Group in Fredericia, Denmark. For more than fifteen years I’ve been building, connecting and operating large scale SAP environments – as a developer, consultant, platform manager, scrum master, team lead and now as an architect.

This blog is where I write about the technical side of that work and about innovation more generally: what works, what doesn’t, and what I learn along the way.

Why this blog

Enterprise software is changing faster than ever – and I want to help shape where it goes.

This website is my space to explore the future of enterprise software technology. AI is changing how systems like SAP are designed, extended and operated, and many assumptions I’ve relied on for years are up for debate. I don’t want to just watch that happen.

  1. 01

    Shape it

    I want to take an active part in how the next generation of enterprise systems is built – from architecture principles to the way teams develop and run them.

  2. 02

    Understand it

    Writing makes me really understand new concepts instead of collecting buzzwords. If I can explain an idea clearly, I’ve understood it.

  3. 03

    Discuss it

    Ideas get better when they’re challenged. I share my thoughts openly and I’m keen to hear other perspectives.

And yes, it’s also about staying relevant: as an expert, I want to keep growing with the technology – especially in the age of AI.

The story so far

  1. 2009 – 2012maihiro (now Accenture) · paricon

    Learning the craft

    My SAP journey started at maihiro in Ismaning, a CRM specialist that is now part of Accenture. I wrote a lot of ABAP there – for clients in the automotive, process and consumer goods industries – and helped build a newsletter marketing add-on that ran inside SAP CRM.

    At paricon in Rosenheim I added a new perspective: supporting pre-sales, turning customer requirements into solution concepts and estimates, and improving data quality for an automotive client’s CRM.

  2. 2012 – 2016HSE Home Shopping Europe · Verlag C.H.BECK

    Switching to the inside

    Next I moved in-house. At HSE Home Shopping Europe I built new features for sales and service in SAP CRM, connected SAP with logistics, e-commerce and TV sales systems, looked after a marketing segmentation app on SAP HANA and helped with a CRM upgrade.

    At the publisher C.H.BECK in Munich I ran requirements and project management for a larger custom development for editor services, built it in ABAP, customised sales and key account management – and trained the people who would use it every day.

  3. 2016 – 2020BMW Group

    Four years with BMW and the first S/4HANA

    At BMW Group I was part of the team behind the company’s first SAP S/4HANA platform, built around stock management and vehicle invoicing. I sized the system, designed integrations with many SAP and non-SAP systems, ran load tests, set up roles and authorisations, checked segregation of duties with SAP GRC and put together a toolset for anonymised test data.

    Later I supported application teams on the platform side – running proofs of concept for integration tools and SAP Landscape Management and planning S/4HANA and BW/4HANA upgrades. In my last role there I led a team of external developers, set up ABAP coding standards and code reviews, and ran agile releases with Large-Scale Scrum.

  4. 2020 – 2023BSH · Siemens · Airbus Defence and Space

    Big transformations

    The next years were all about large S/4HANA programmes. At BSH I supported an international greenfield rollout in sales and condition contract settlement. At Siemens I coordinated ten agile product teams in an S/4HANA Finance transformation and was Scrum Master for Central Finance and for connecting a non-SAP procurement platform.

    At Airbus Defence and Space I helped set up a new ERP landscape with S/4HANA and SAP EWM, took over service ownership for EWM, connected S/4HANA with SAP Analytics Cloud and supported the audit-compliant retirement of old systems.

  5. 2023 – 2026the LEGO Group, Billund

    Leading teams at LEGO

    In 2023 I joined the LEGO Group in Billund. First I led a multinational product team for tax and trade compliance – around 50 applications on SAP ERP and SAP GTS, covering things like tax determination, e-invoicing and international trade.

    Then I took over SAP Governance and Enablement with a team of seven engineers: stream lead in the S/4HANA programme, owner of the integration and clean-core development strategy, and responsible for security with SAP GRC, data retention with SAP ILM and test automation with SAP Cloud ALM and Tricentis Tosca.

  6. 2026 – todayDLG Group, Fredericia

    Architecture at DLG Group

    Since March 2026 I’ve been an SAP Technical Architect at DLG Group in Fredericia. My job is to keep the SAP landscape running well and to make sure it grows in the right direction – together with our own SAP teams, external partners and the enterprise architecture boards.
